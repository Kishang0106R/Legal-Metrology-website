# User Manual

## Government

Sign in with email OTP. Use Dashboard to select a business and product, create an inspection, assign an inspector, upload evidence, run OCR, review extracted fields, run compliance rules, record an officer decision, create samples, follow laboratory work, create confirmed violations, and generate reports.

## Laboratory

Use Assigned Samples to verify receipt and custody. Create tests, enter measured results, and generate a laboratory report for review. Laboratory results do not automatically create violations.

## Business

Use My Business and My Products to maintain permitted records. View inspection, compliance, and report information that belongs to the business. Submit corrective action when requested.

## Legal decision boundary

OCR and automated compliance findings are assistance only. A confirmed violation requires an authorized officer decision and remains traceable through history and audit logs.
